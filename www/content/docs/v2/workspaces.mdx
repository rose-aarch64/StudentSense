---
title: Workspaces
description: Keep separate sets of sources, chats and Studio outputs apart
---

A workspace keeps one research context together: its sources and folders, its chats, and what Studio made from them. A question in one workspace never searches another.

Your models, plugins, license and settings are not part of a workspace. They apply to every workspace on this computer.

Step-by-step guide, with a video: [Keep projects apart with workspaces](/docs/v2/guides/workspaces).

## Your first workspace

The first time SurfSense starts, it makes a workspace called **My Workspace**, so there is always one to open. You can rename it or add more.

## The workspace rail

Workspaces are listed in the rail at the far left of the window. Each row shows the workspace's icon, or the first letters of its name when it has no icon, followed by its name. Click a workspace to switch to it. SurfSense remembers the last workspace you opened and returns to it the next time you start the app.

Switching does not stop anything. A reply still being written in another workspace keeps going, and its chat is marked unread when it finishes.

## Create a workspace

1. Click **New** below the list in the rail, then choose **Workspace**.
2. Type a name.
3. Click **Create**.

The new workspace opens straight away, empty. A name can be up to 200 characters.

## Rename a workspace

Right-click the workspace in the rail and choose **Rename**. Type the new name and click **Rename**.

## Give a workspace an icon

Right-click the workspace in the rail and choose **Icon…**. Pick an icon from the grid and click **Save**. Choose **None** to go back to the workspace's first letters.

## Group workspaces in folders

A folder groups workspaces in the rail, such as one folder for each term's classes. Workspaces that are in no folder are listed first, then each folder with its workspaces under it. Folders do not nest.

- **Make a folder.** Click **New** below the list in the rail, then choose **Folder**. Type a name and click **Create**. The folder starts empty. You can also right-click a workspace, choose **Move to folder**, then **New folder…**, which moves that workspace into the new folder.
- **Move a workspace.** Right-click it, choose **Move to folder**, and pick a folder. Pick **No folder** to take it out of its folder.
- **Close or open a folder.** Click the folder's row. SurfSense remembers which folders you closed, on this computer.
- **Rename a folder.** Right-click the folder and choose **Rename**.
- **Delete a folder.** Right-click the folder, choose **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete folder**. Its workspaces are kept and move out of the folder.

If you connect Canvas, each course's workspace is put in a folder named for its term. A workspace you move to another folder stays where you put it.

## Delete a workspace

Right-click the workspace in the rail and choose **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete workspace**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a workspace permanently deletes its chats, its sources, their
  indexed data and its Studio outputs. Replies still being written in it are
  stopped first. There is no undo and no Trash.
</Callout>

Your original files elsewhere on your computer are not touched: SurfSense deletes only its own copies.
